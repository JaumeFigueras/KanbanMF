User manual
===========

This part of the documentation is for the people who *use* KanbanMF: how to
organise work in boards, lists and cards, share it with others, get reminded
of due dates and keep track of the time you spend. If you are installing or
developing KanbanMF, see :doc:`../setup/index` instead.

The screenshots show a demo account redesigning a website with two
colleagues.

Words used in this manual
-------------------------

Board
   A project or area of work: a row of lists side by side. You see every board
   you own or that someone has shared with you.

List
   A column on a board, usually a stage of work such as *To do*,
   *In progress* or *Done*.

Card
   One piece of work, sitting in a list. A card has a name and, optionally, a
   description, dates, labels, people and checklists. Moving a card from list
   to list is how work moves forward.

Checklist
   A named list of items to tick off inside a card. A card can have several.

Label
   A coloured tag, such as *Bug* or *Urgent*, defined once per board and
   attached to any of its cards.

Owner
   The person who created a board. Only the owner can rename, share, archive
   or delete it, and manage its labels.

Member and assignee
   People on a card. *Members* are involved in it; *assignees* are the people
   responsible for getting it done, and the only ones who can receive due-date
   reminders for it.

Template
   A board that holds only lists, used as a starting point for new boards.

Time entry
   A record, private to you, of a stretch of time you spent on a card.

A quick tour
------------

After signing in you arrive at the boards overview, which lists your boards
in sections: the ones you starred, the ones you own, the ones shared with you,
and your templates.

.. figure:: img/boards-overview.png
   :alt: The boards overview, with the Starred, My Boards, Shared with me and
         Templates sections.

   The boards overview.

Click a board to open it. Its lists run from left to right, and each card
shows its labels, its due date — coloured by how close it is — checklist
progress, and the people on it.

.. figure:: img/board.png
   :alt: A board with four lists of cards.

   A board.

The bar at the top is the same on every screen. Click **KanbanMF** to go back
to the boards overview, the timer icon to open the :doc:`time tracker
<time_tracker>`, your name to open the user menu (your profile, preferences
and theme — see :doc:`accounts` and :doc:`preferences`), and the last icon to
sign out.

Whatever you change appears straight away for anyone else who has the same
board open, and their changes appear for you — there is no need to reload the
page.
